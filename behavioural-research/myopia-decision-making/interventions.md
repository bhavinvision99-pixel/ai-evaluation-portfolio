# Intervention design

## Layer 1: pre-consultation

Content was mapped to See-Think-Do-Care stages.

The intended behavioural functions were to reduce uncertainty, give parents a basis for comparison and allow authority to arrive through third-party sources before the consultation.

## Layer 2: structured consultation

A fixed-sequence consultation protocol was designed so that concern was addressed before necessity was increased.

The protocol also acted as an intervention on the clinician by reducing the need to improvise how strongly to push a recommendation.

## Layer 3: decision point

Fogg B=MAP, MINDSPACE and EAST were used to examine:

- timing of the prompt
- messenger
- salience
- defaults
- commitment
- friction across booking and forms

The case study also describes an explicit ethical limit on fear-based persuasion.

## Layer 4: adherence

The adherence layer focused on:

- child capability and confidence
- reinforcement
- contact independent of sales
- visible progress feedback

The project treats long-term adherence as a behavioural outcome rather than simply a commercial retention metric.
