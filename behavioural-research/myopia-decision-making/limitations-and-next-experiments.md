# Limitations and next experiments

## Limitations

- Mechanisms were not measured quantitatively.
- The intervention combined multiple behavioural components.
- Parents who declined were under-studied.
- Discontinuation reasons were not coded systematically.
- Practice data were observational.
- The child was initially under-weighted as an independent decision agent.
- The clinician-side intervention was discovered relatively late.

## Proposed next experiments

1. Measure necessity and concern before and after consultation.
2. Compare the structured protocol with a control consultation.
3. Include families who decline in follow-up research.
4. Code discontinuation reasons prospectively.
5. Separate and test selected intervention components where practical.
6. Pre-specify behaviour-change techniques and outcomes.
