# Outcomes and interpretation

## Observed outcomes

- approximately 80% consultation-to-enrolment conversion
- under 6% attrition across four years
- no paid advertising during the growth period
- clinic capacity reported as one to two months ahead
- observational practice data: 79% of children seen in their first year showed no measurable prescription change

## Interpretation

These outcomes show that the service achieved strong conversion and retention under constrained conditions.

They do **not** prove that any single behavioural mechanism caused the result.

The source case study explicitly identifies this limitation and proposes stronger measurement and controlled comparison as the next research step.
