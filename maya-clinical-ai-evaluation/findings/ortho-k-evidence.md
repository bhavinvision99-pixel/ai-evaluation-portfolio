# Ortho-k infection-rate source tracing

A parent-facing article used a serious-infection figure of roughly **4–6 per 10,000** for ortho-k without citing the source. The knowledge base separately cited:

- a US retrospective study at roughly **14 per 10,000 patient-years** in children
- a Russian study at roughly **5 per 10,000**

On first review, the figures appeared to conflict.

Source tracing showed that the article's 4–6 per 10,000 figure was drawn from the same Russian study already cited in the knowledge base. The knowledge base did not need correction; the article could cite the underlying study directly.

This finding is documented as **WE-02** in the evaluation workbook.
