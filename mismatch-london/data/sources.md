# Data Sources

## ONS Census 2021

Dataset used:
**Occupancy rating (bedrooms) by tenure — Census 2021**

Used for:
- social-rented household counts
- overcrowding rates
- under-occupation rates
- borough-level comparison
- London benchmark

Key definitions:
- overcrowded = occupancy rating `−1 or less`
- under-occupied 1+ = `+1` plus `+2 or more`
- strongly under-occupied = `+2 or more`

## Greater London Authority

GLA affordable-housing programme data was reviewed for:
- Social Rent / London Affordable Rent starts
- completions by borough
- London-wide bedroom mix
- council-home bedroom mix

The currently available files did not provide the full combination of:
**borough × year × tenure × bedroom size**

That remains an important data gap for the next build.

## MHCLG / Local Authority Housing Statistics

Used for:
- local-authority waiting-list totals by borough

Waiting-list figures should be interpreted cautiously because boroughs can differ in eligibility and register-management practices.

## Future datasets to add

Priority additions:
1. Waiting-list bedroom requirement by borough
2. Borough-level bedroom mix of newly delivered social housing
3. Age and household composition associated with overcrowding and under-occupation

These would allow MISMATCH to move from a diagnosis of housing patterns toward a richer understanding of whether existing stock, registered demand and new delivery appear aligned.
