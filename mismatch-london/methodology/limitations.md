# Limitations

MISMATCH is an exploratory policy-intelligence project. It should not be used as an automated policy recommendation system.

## Data limitations

- Census occupancy data is from 2021.
- Occupancy rating is a calculated bedroom standard and cannot represent all individual preferences or circumstances.
- A bedroom counted as additional capacity may be used for caring, disability, family or other needs not visible in the dataset.
- Under-occupation does not establish willingness to move.
- Under-occupation does not establish ability to move.
- Borough-level averages do not describe individual households.

## Waiting-list limitations

Local-authority housing-register figures are not perfectly comparable across boroughs.

Councils can differ in:
- eligibility criteria
- qualification rules
- review processes
- register maintenance
- treatment of housing-association lists
- duplicate registrations

Waiting-list totals are therefore described as **registered housing demand**, not a definitive measure of housing need.

## Methodological limitations

- Relative Imbalance is an exploratory diagnostic measure.
- The ±12.5% threshold is a modelling choice, not an official standard.
- Borough classification does not imply a preferred intervention.
- Statistical significance does not automatically imply policy significance.
- Correlation does not establish causation.

## Policy limitations

The current analysis cannot determine:
- whether residents want to move
- whether suitable alternative homes exist
- whether relocation would improve resident outcomes
- whether voluntary mobility would reduce waiting-list pressure
- which intervention would be most cost-effective
- whether new supply, mobility, allocation reform or another intervention is most appropriate

## Human-impact limitation

Housing stock is not an abstract optimisation system.

Every observed household represents a home, community and individual set of circumstances.

For that reason MISMATCH deliberately avoids converting borough-level patterns into household-level recommendations.
