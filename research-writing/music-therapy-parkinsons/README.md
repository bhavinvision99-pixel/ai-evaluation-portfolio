## Music Therapy in Parkinson’s Disease

**Type:** Independent narrative literature review
**Focus:** Evidence evaluation, clinical research and critical appraisal

An independent review of the evidence for music-based interventions in Parkinson’s disease. It examines rhythmic auditory stimulation, gait, speech, mood, cognition and quality of life, as well as the use of music therapy alongside medication and multidisciplinary rehabilitation.

The review compares evidence from systematic reviews, meta-analyses and clinical studies. It also considers weaknesses in the evidence base, including small samples, inconsistent interventions, short follow-up periods and limited evidence involving people with advanced Parkinson’s disease.

**Key skills demonstrated:** literature searching, evidence synthesis, critical appraisal, scientific writing and cautious interpretation of clinical findings.
