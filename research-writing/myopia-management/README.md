# Defusing the Myopia Time-Bomb

## Type of work

Earlier clinical evidence-synthesis and scientific writing.

The paper was prepared for *The Ophthalmologist* and reviews the rising prevalence of myopia, associated long-term ocular risks, possible mechanisms of onset and progression, risk prediction, and intervention options.

## What the paper demonstrates

- synthesis of a broad clinical literature
- comparison of competing mechanisms and treatments
- use of quantitative risk estimates
- translation of scientific evidence into clinical recommendations
- scientific writing for a professional audience
- extensive referencing across epidemiology, optics, pharmacology and clinical management

## Scope

The paper discusses:

- prevalence and long-term ocular risk
- genetic and environmental factors
- accommodation and vergence
- peripheral retinal defocus
- prediction of myopia onset and progression
- atropine
- vision training
- orthokeratology
- soft contact lenses
- myopia-control spectacle lenses
- parent-facing behavioural advice

## Important note

This is included as evidence of previous research synthesis and scientific communication.

The paper reflects the evidence base and clinical context at the time it was written. It should not be treated as current clinical guidance, and no attempt has been made here to silently update older scientific claims.
